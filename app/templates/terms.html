Terms of Use

Basic rules for participating in SkillBridge.

These terms summarize the expected standards of behavior for people who create accounts, publish skills, participate in exchanges, and submit reviews on SkillBridge.

Acceptable use

Users should provide accurate account information, use the platform for legitimate educational or skill-sharing purposes, and avoid impersonation, fraud, harassment, malicious conduct, or misuse of another person's identity.

Account responsibility

Each user is responsible for activities performed through their account. Users should keep their login credentials secure and should not knowingly allow another person to operate the platform under their identity.

Skill listings and profile information

Skill listings, profile descriptions, and session notes should be reasonably truthful and relevant to the platform. Users should not post misleading qualifications, fabricated experience, or content intended to manipulate other participants.

Session integrity

Exchange requests, confirmations, and credit settlements should reflect real interactions. Users must not falsely confirm sessions, abuse the time-credit system, or attempt to manipulate balances or completion states.

Reviews and conduct

Reviews should be relevant to the completed exchange and free of harassment, threats, deception, hate speech, or abusive behavior. The review system is intended to improve trust, not to provide a space for personal attacks.

Platform changes and availability

SkillBridge is an evolving platform. Features, workflows, and availability may change over time as the service is improved. The maintainers may update functionality, modify exchange behavior, or temporarily restrict access when necessary for maintenance, security, or reliability reasons.

Misuse and restriction

Accounts or activity that appear fraudulent, abusive, deceptive, or harmful to the integrity of the platform may be restricted, removed, or reviewed by the maintainers of the service.
