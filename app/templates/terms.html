Terms of Use

Basic rules for participating in SkillBridge.

These terms summarize expected behavior for users who create accounts, exchange skills, and submit reviews on the platform.

Acceptable use

Users should provide accurate account information, use the platform for legitimate educational or skill-sharing purposes, and avoid impersonation, fraud, or misuse of another person's identity.

Session integrity

Credits and completion confirmations should reflect real sessions. Users should not falsely confirm sessions or manipulate reviews or balances.

Reviews and conduct

Reviews should be relevant to the completed exchange and free of harassment, abuse, or deceptive statements.
