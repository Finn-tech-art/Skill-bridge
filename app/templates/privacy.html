Privacy Policy

How SkillBridge handles account and profile data.

This page explains the basic information used to operate the platform and the kinds of activity records visible inside the product.

Information we collect

We collect the account details you provide during signup, profile information you add voluntarily, your listed skills, session activity, review content, and credit ledger activity needed to operate the platform.

Why we use it

Your information is used to authenticate your account, match you with relevant peers, track session activity, and maintain credit and review history inside SkillBridge.

Third-party services

Authentication and data storage are supported through Supabase, and the application is hosted on Render.
