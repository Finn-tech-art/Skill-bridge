Privacy Policy

How SkillBridge handles account and profile data.

This page explains what information SkillBridge collects, how that information is used to operate the platform, and what activity records are stored as part of the skill-exchange workflow.

Information we collect

SkillBridge collects the information you provide during account creation and platform use. This includes your email address, name, username, profile information such as department, year of study, and bio, your listed skills and learning goals, exchange session activity, review content, and credit ledger records required to track platform exchanges.

Why the information is used

Your information is used to authenticate your account, display your profile to other users inside the platform, generate relevant match recommendations, create and manage exchange sessions, support review and reputation features, and maintain credit history where time-credit exchanges are involved.

Authentication and platform infrastructure

SkillBridge uses Supabase for email/password authentication and data storage, and the application is hosted on Render. As a result, account and product data are processed through those supporting services as part of normal platform operation.

What other users can see

Other users of the platform may be able to see your public-facing profile details, the skills you offer, the skills you want to learn, and the reviews associated with completed exchanges. Internal activity such as private login credentials is not displayed to other users.

Credit and review records

SkillBridge maintains ledger and review records because the platform depends on accountability. Credit transactions, session completion confirmations, and review outcomes form part of the historical record of how exchanges occurred on the platform.

Data accuracy and user responsibility

Users are responsible for providing accurate information and keeping their profile details reasonably up to date. False or misleading account information can affect platform trust, matching quality, and exchange fairness.

Current limitations

SkillBridge is still evolving. While reasonable steps are being taken to support secure authentication and account handling, the platform is not yet claiming enterprise-grade compliance or formal legal certification. Users should avoid posting unnecessary sensitive personal information in free-text fields.
