About SkillBridge

A peer-learning marketplace for student communities.

SkillBridge is a web platform designed to help university students teach, learn, and exchange practical skills in a more structured way than ad hoc chats, informal study groups, or one-off word-of-mouth arrangements.

Purpose Help students teach, learn, and build trust through accountable, trackable exchanges.
Model Direct swaps and time-credit sessions with clear completion confirmation and review history.

What SkillBridge does

Structured skill exchange for real student needs

Students can create profiles, list skills they can teach, add skills they want to learn, browse available peer expertise, review ranked matches, request exchange sessions, confirm completed sessions, and leave reviews after an exchange has taken place.

How trust is built

Visible reputation, confirmations, and ledger history

SkillBridge uses user reviews, double-confirmation for completed sessions, and a visible credit ledger to make the learning relationship more transparent over time. The goal is to reduce ambiguity around whether a session actually happened and whether value was exchanged fairly.

Who the platform is for

Students, peer tutors, and campus communities

SkillBridge is intended for student communities where learners may also be teachers. A user might teach design, writing, coding, languages, public speaking, music, research methods, or any other practical skill that another student wants to acquire.

Why it exists

Making informal skill sharing more discoverable and fair

Many students already share skills informally, but informal arrangements make it hard to discover the right person, track commitments, or build a record of trust. SkillBridge introduces searchable skill profiles, exchange types, review visibility, and credit tracking so peer learning becomes easier to coordinate and easier to evaluate.

Current status

Active prototype moving toward production readiness

The current version of SkillBridge is an actively evolving platform. It already supports hosted authentication, profile creation, skill management, match recommendations, exchange requests, review submission, and a credit ledger. Some advanced features such as richer scheduling, stronger moderation, and additional security controls are still planned improvements.
