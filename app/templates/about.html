About SkillBridge

A peer-learning marketplace for student communities.

SkillBridge is designed to help students exchange practical skills in a more structured way than ad hoc messaging groups or informal arrangements.

Purpose Help students teach, learn, and build trust through accountable exchanges.
Model Direct swaps and time-credit sessions with clear completion confirmation.

What the platform does

Structured skill exchange

Students can list what they teach, what they want to learn, discover ranked partners, request sessions, and review each other after completed exchanges.

How trust is built

Visible reputation and credit history

SkillBridge uses reviews, confirmation states, and credit ledger entries to make exchanges more transparent over time.
